# Problem size: impersonation and fake-authority scams in India (as of 31 Oct 2026)

Method note: all figures below were found via web search snippets and a few page fetches. I could not open any primary government document (PIB, sansad.in and rsdebate.nic.in returned redirects or no results). Every government figure is therefore tagged [SECONDARY] = reported by a news or aggregator outlet quoting MHA/I4C/Parliament, not read in the primary document. Primary sources to pull for verification: Lok Sabha unstarred question AU1560 (Dec 2025), Rajya Sabha PQ 267 of 12 Mar 2025, Lok Sabha answers of 22 Jul 2025 and 2 Dec 2025.

## 1. Official loss and complaint figures, 2022 to 2026

### Takeaway
NCRP-reported losses rose from about Rs 2,290 crore (2022) to about Rs 22,500 crore (2024 and 2025), roughly a 10x rise in three years, while complaints grew about 2.7x. 2025 losses were flat versus 2024 in rupees but complaints rose about 24%. The 2025 totals conflict across sources (Rs 19,813 vs 22,495 crore; 24 lakh vs 28 lakh complaints).

### Inferences
- Official data is count-heavy but not tied to "fake authority" as a category; the closest proxy is digital arrest. Roughly 1.2% of complaints reach FIR level (3% on the cumulative figure), so reported losses understate true harm.
- The 2025 loss plateau with rising complaint counts implies average loss per complaint fell (about Rs 1.0 lakh in 2024 vs about Rs 0.8 lakh in 2025 on ThePrint's numbers; my calculation).

### Gaps
- No primary-document read for any figure above. 2022 and 2023 rows are single-outlet. No official 2026 YTD national total.

## 2. Breakdown by scam type

### Takeaway
By money, investment/trading fraud dominates (about 76% of 2025 losses); digital arrest is about 9% of money. By count, investment is about 35%, sextortion 19%, digital arrest 6%. Digital arrest is the archetypal fake-authority scam but is not the biggest by rupees; fake stock tips and fake brokers are.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 shares (MHA data): investment fraud 76% of money / 35% of cases (about Rs 17,096 crore, 9.85 lakh cases, derived); digital arrest 9% of money / 6% of cases (about Rs 2,025 crore, 1.69 lakh cases, derived); sextortion 4% of money / 19% of cases; other about 11% money / 40% cases (derived). [SECONDARY] — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/cybercrime-saw-24-spike-in-2025-indians-lost-rs-22495-crore-mainly-in-investment-scams/2859930/)
- Digital arrest: 2022 to Feb 2025: more than 2.4 lakh complaints, Rs 2,576 crore; 2024 peak with 1.2 lakh complaints / Rs 1,935 crore (Lok Sabha/RS reply 12 Mar 2025). [SECONDARY] — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1080247/over-38-lakh-cyber-fraud-complaints-since-2021-most-from-up-maharashtra-centre); [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/03/12/del54-rsq-cybercrime.html)
- Digital arrest 2024 CONFLICT: Rs 1,935 crore (MHA, above) vs Rs 1,616 crore from 63,481 complaints (another report in search results) vs 1,23,672 complaints in 2024 (SC proceedings, above). Also CONFLICT for 2025: 58,249 complaints (SC proceedings) vs about 1.69 lakh implied by ThePrint's 6% share. Unresolved; likely different definitions.
- Digital arrest Jan to Feb 2025: 17,718 cases, Rs 210.21 crore. [SECONDARY] — search summary of MHA reply (The Week/PTI)
- Jan to Apr 2024 (I4C): trading scams Rs 1,420.48 crore, investment scams Rs 222.58 crore, digital arrest about Rs 120 crore, romance/dating Rs 13.23 crore. [SECONDARY] — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/news/indians-lost-rs-120-crore-in-digital-arrest-frauds-in-first-quarter-of-2024-report)
- Supreme Court said digital-arrest victims, mostly elderly, lost almost Rs 3,000 crore (Oct/Nov 2025); roughly in line with the MHA Rs 2,576 crore cumulative. [SECONDARY] — [The Hindu via Superkalam](https://superkalam.com/current-affairs/04-11-2025/digital-arrests-are-a-very-big-challenge-victims-mostly-elderly-lost-3-000-cr-in-india-alone-sc-pg14-e9a40d2b-a355-49c7-ae2f-a819ae7fb574)
- Survey view (GSMA India Consumer Scam Report 2026, n=500, consumer survey not official): 12% scammed in past 12 months (10% in 2025 edition); online shopping 33%, prize/lottery/inheritance 31%, job/income 30%, QR/payment-link 27%; channels: messaging apps 42%, social media 37%, email 33%; 89% saw AI used in scams; 33% of victims recovered some money; 86% reported. — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/news/12-per-cent-of-indian-respondents-scammed-in-past-year-gsma)
- Fake jobs: LocalCircles (May 2025, 22,000+ respondents, 312 districts): at least 42% of WhatsApp users received fake job offers. [SECONDARY] — [Angel One summary](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/what-is-the-localcircles-survey-is-saying-about-whatsapp-scams-in-india)
- Stock-tip groups: SEBI press release (Oct 2026) warned against private WhatsApp tip groups run by unregistered people, often from foreign numbers, using fabricated member endorsements ("astroturfing"). — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/sebi-cautions-investors-against-fraudulent-stock-tip-whatsapp-groups) [SECONDARY, press release not opened]
- Illegal loan apps: no current national count found. RBI working group (2021) found 600 of 1,100 digital lending apps illegal; Google removed 2,500+ (Apr 2021 to Jul 2022) and 2,200+ (Sep 2022 to Aug 2023) loan apps per MoS Finance. [SECONDARY, old] — [Google removes 2,200 apps](https://www.cxodigitalpulse.com/google-removes-2200-fraudulent-loan-apps-from-play-store-mos-finance/); [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/more-than-half-indian-loan-apps-illegal-rbi-panel-finds-a-17970)
- Karnataka digital arrest: 874 cases / Rs 151.25 crore (2024), 293 cases / Rs 144.59 crore (2025). [SECONDARY, state] — [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/12/19/mes20-ka-digital-arrest-minister.html)

### Inferences
- A tool aimed at "fake stock tip / fake adviser" verification addresses the largest money category; a tool aimed at fake notices/digital arrest addresses the most emotionally salient and court-visible category but about 9% of rupees.
- Digital arrest complaints appear to be falling in 2025/26 per SC proceedings, possibly due to awareness and enforcement; investment fraud is the persistent category.

### Gaps
- No official split for "fake government/legal/tax notice" (income tax, customs/courier, TRAI) as separate from digital arrest. No 2025 or 2026 official figures for illegal loan apps or fake jobs by money.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 NCRP (Rajya Sabha reply): 1,03,488 senior-citizen complaints, Rs 4,005.12 crore; 4,63,114 women's complaints, Rs 3,764.51 crore; combined Rs 7,769.63 crore (the two groups may overlap). ## 4. How people verify today

### Takeaway
Verification is mostly informal and weak. Survey data on who people turn to is thin; the best Indian data point is only 27% of GSMA victims call back on official numbers. No survey found giving a clean "ask family / Google / call bank / ignore" split.

### Cited Findings
- GSMA 2026 (n=500, India): 27% called companies back on official numbers rather than trust incoming contact; 49% strengthened account security; 61% avoided some inbound contact or transactions; 72% changed behaviour; 65% of reported cases saw an organisation take responsibility, 33% unresolved. — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/news/12-per-cent-of-indian-respondents-scammed-in-past-year-gsma)
- McAfee State of the Scamiverse 2026 (survey Nov 2025, 7,592 adults across countries; India subset size not captured): 51% of Indians report losing money to a scam, average loss Rs 93,915; 13 scam messages per day; 24% of victims were targeted again within a year. Self-reported and vendor-run, so likely inflated vs official data. — [McAfee press release via search](https://www.mcafee.com/en-in/consumer-corporate/newsroom/press-releases/2026/20260204-1.html); [Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/general/indians-get-13-scam-messages-daily-ai-and-deepfake-boosting-scams.html/amp/)
- McAfee 2023: 82% of Indians said they fell for or clicked fake messages; average 12 fake messages daily; 1h45m per week spent verifying message authenticity. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/an-average-indian-gets-12-fake-messages-daily-and-these-are-the-most-common-ones-they-fall-prey-to-study-405067-2023-11-08)
- Truecaller: over 4,000 crore spam calls received in India in 2025, 1,189 crore blocked. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/indians-received-over-4000-crore-spam-calls-in-2025-1189-crore-blocked-report-515765-2026-02-11)
- LocalCircles Feb 2024: about 90% of respondents get pesky calls despite DND. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/90-respondents-getting-pesky-calls-despite-being-in-dnd-list-localcircles-124021800429_1.html)
- Official advice channels: 1930 helpline and cybercrime.gov.in; SEBI says verify adviser registration (INA, INH, INZ prefixes) on sebi.gov.in. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/sebi-circular-on-frauds-verify-registration-before-taking-investment-tips-125042000636_1.html)

### Inferences
- Existing verification is fragmented across SEBI's registry, RBI lists, etc., each requiring the user to know where to look; this is the gap a forward-and-verify agent fills. This is my inference, not a cited finding.

### Gaps
- No survey found on first-resort verification behaviour (family vs Google vs bank vs ignore). GSMA India report PDF is image-only and could not be read (full report at [GSMA](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/asia-pacific/gsma_resources/india-consumer-scam-report/)); McAfee India subset not extracted; NPCI and government survey data not found.

## 5. Recent policy signals, 2025 to 2026

### Takeaway
The Supreme Court is actively supervising a suo motu digital arrest case (since Oct 2025) that moved to a CBI probe (Dec 2025) and nationwide directions (Aug 2026). SEBI is tightening rules on adviser/finfluencer identification and warning on WhatsApp tip groups. Most items are verified only via news.

### Cited Findings
- 17 Oct 2025: SC took suo motu cognisance of digital arrest after a letter from an elderly Ambala couple (loss reported as Rs 1.05 crore, other reports Rs 1.5 crore: conflict); bench of Justices Surya Kant and Joymalya Bagchi. 27 Oct 2025: notices to all States/UTs for FIR details. — [The Leaflet](https://theleaflet.in/leaflet-reports/supreme-court-seeks-response-from-all-states-uts-on-digital-arrest-scams-mulls-cbi-probe); [Hitavada](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2025/10/28/all-states-uts-get-sc-notices-in-suo-moto-case-on-digital-arrest-scams.html)
- 1 Dec 2025: SC ordered CBI to investigate digital arrest scams and asked non-consenting States to consent; also asked RBI why AI is not used to identify/freeze mule accounts. — [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-orders-cbi-probe-into-digital-arrest-scams)
- Jan 2026: MeitY met Google, Meta, Microsoft (6 Jan); DoT SIM-binding directives being pursued; Centre told SC a panel was formed with DoT and RBI inputs. WhatsApp told SC it banned 9,400+ accounts tied to digital arrest scams in a 12-week window from Jan 2026. I4C says 80 to 82% of mule accounts operate for under one day; I4C–RBI MuleHunter.ai MoU. — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/whatsapp-banned-9400-accounts-used-for-digital-arrest-scams-in-2026-ag-tells-supreme-court/80326.html); [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-home-affairs-ministry-report-apex-court-sop-digital-arrests/)
- Early Aug 2026: SC issued nationwide directions: RBI to frame mule-account SOP within 4 weeks; MeitY/DoT to assess feasibility of time-based limits on voice/video calls ("kill switch"); States/UTs to operationalise grievance and fund-restoration mechanisms; time-bound refunds. — [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-issues-directions-to-curb-digital-arrest-scams-asks-rbi-to-frame-sop-on-mule-accounts); [Vision IAS](https://visionias.in/current-affairs/news-today/2026-08-05/security/supreme-court-mandates-sops-to-curb-digital-arrests-ensure-time-bound-refunds) [SECONDARY; order itself not read]
- SEBI: Apr 2025 circular urging investors to verify registration; Feb 2026 rule requiring registered name and SEBI registration number on social handles and securities posts; Mar 2026 SEBI–Google verified badges for registered trading apps and AI-based finfluencer monitoring; Oct 2026 WhatsApp tip-group warning. [SECONDARY] — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/sebi-circular-on-frauds-verify-registration-before-taking-investment-tips-125042000636_1.html); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/sebi-cautions-investors-against-fraudulent-stock-tip-whatsapp-groups); Feb/Mar 2026 items from a search summary (original outlet not captured)
- Government debunked a fake India Post survey offering Rs 20,000 (Aug 2026), an example of PIB-type fact-checks. — [News on AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/govt-debunks-fake-india-post-survey-offering-%e2%82%b920000-cash-prize/)

### Inferences
- Regulators are building supply-side controls (mule accounts, platform bans, adviser badges) but nothing found gives an individual a one-step "is this real" check across registries; consistent with the product premise but not proof of it.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed whether RBI issued the mule-account SOP by its early-Sept 2026 deadline. No evidence found on MeitY/DoT action beyond meetings and directives. No CBIC, Income Tax or PIB Fact Check specific scam statistics found. Latest SC hearing status after Aug 2026 unknown.
