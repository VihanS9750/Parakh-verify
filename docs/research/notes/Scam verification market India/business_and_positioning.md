## 3. Regulatory tailwinds and constraints

### Takeaway
Regulators are pushing "verify the entity" behaviour (RBI app directory, SEBI registered-intermediary lists), which Parakh automates. The main risks are labelling a named firm "fraud" (defamation / RBI-style natural-justice norms) and handling personal data in forwarded documents (DPDP).

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025 (8 May 2025): regulated entities report digital lending apps via CIMS; RBI publishes a public list of DLAs (target 1 Jul 2025) so borrowers can confirm an app is recognised. Sources differ on whether the list is verified by RBI or only CCO-certified — [RBI press release](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60403); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/banking/rbi-directs-banks-res-to-submit-digital-lending-app-information-on-cims-portal); [CBCL NLIU](https://cbcl.nliu.ac.in/contemporary-issues/analysing-rbis-digital-lending-directions-2025-a-positive-step-towards-responsible-lending/)
- [FACT] RBI Master Directions on Fraud Risk Management (15 Jul 2024): before a bank classifies an account as fraud it must issue a show-cause notice with at least 21 days to respond, per natural justice. This is the regulator's own standard on labelling someone fraudulent — [AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/master-directions-on-fraud-risk-management-in-banks-and-nbfcs); [Outlook Money](https://outlookmoney.com/banking/banks-can-only-tag-accounts-as-fraudulent-after-seeking-borrowers-response-rbi)
- [FACT] SEBI Aug 2024 amendments bar regulated entities from associating with unregistered finfluencers (education-only exception); SEBI had 15,000+ pieces of unregulated content taken down — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-amends-rules-to-regulate-finfluencers); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/investment/story/sebi-tweaks-regulations-for-finfluencers-removes-content-of-over-15000-unregulated-entities-443789-2024-08-31)
- [FACT] SEBI's site hosts the registered-intermediaries list; SEBI warned of forged SEBI-letterhead STT notices (Feb 2026), fake apps, and fake registration certificates — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/sebi-warns-investors-about-fake-stt-notices-account-handling-services--20260227153139); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/exchanges-alert-investors-to-fake-trading-apps-and-impersonators)
- [FACT] DPDP Rules 2025 notified 13/14 Nov 2025; most obligations (Rules 3, 5-16, 22, 23) apply after 18 months, around 12/13 May 2027 (sources differ by a day); Board provisions commenced on notification. So at demo time (Oct 2026) the core duties are not yet in force but will be within ~6 months — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/breaking-govt-notifies-final-dpdp-rules-sets-staggered-rollout-with-key-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries-84201.htm); [TCSA](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap); [AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/?p=87799)
- [FACT] IT Rules 2023 amendment creating a government Fact Check Unit (PIB Fact Check designated) was struck down by Bombay HC (Sept 2024); in Mar 2026 the Supreme Court agreed to hear the Centre's appeal but refused a stay, with a 3-judge bench to decide. Final outcome not found — [Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/technology-law-analysis/bombay-high-court-brings-fact-check-unit-saga-to-a-close-delivers-tie-breaker-judgment-striking-down-fact-check-unit-set-up-under-it-rules-as-unconstitutional-15155); [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/court-updates/supreme-court/refused-stay-bombay-high-court-order-striking-down-it-rules-2023-fact-check-units-1609570); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/paramount-importance-supreme-court-3judge-bench-to-decide-validity-of-fact-check-units/79892.html)

### Inferences (what to avoid saying or storing) [MY JUDGEMENT, not legal advice]
- Say "not found in the official registry as of [timestamp], source: [link]" and "matches known scam pattern X", never "X is a fraud/scam company". Verdict classes such as REGISTERED / NOT FOUND / MISMATCH / PATTERN-MATCH / CANNOT VERIFY, each with a dated source link.
- Registry absence is not proof of fraud (stale lists, name variants, CCO-certified-not-verified DLA list). Tell the user so.
- PIB Fact Check: cite it as one source among several, not as legal authority; the government FCU rule is struck down and under appeal. Do not present it as the arbiter of truth.
- Data: do not persist forwarded images/documents or phone numbers; process in memory, store only a hash plus verdict, auto-delete; redact Aadhaar/PAN/account numbers before sending to any LLM; explicit notice and consent line in-chat; no sale of data. Align early with DPDP purpose limitation and erasure (obligations bite ~May 2027).
- Never tell users to pay, invest, or avoid paying; next step is "verify via official channel / report at cybercrime.gov.in or 1930 / Chakshu". (Reporting channels per [Factly](https://factly.in/explainer-government-launches-chakshu-to-report-sms-call-or-whatsapp-messages-attempting-to-commit-fraud/amp); 1930 helpline from general knowledge, verify.)

## 6. Positioning and the three hardest judge questions [MY JUDGEMENT throughout]

### Takeaway
Position Parakh as the "check before you pay" layer: it does not decide if something is a scam from vibes; it looks it up in the official registry and shows the receipt.

### Cited Findings
- (Context) Regulators already publish the registries and tell the public to verify (RBI DLA list, SEBI intermediary list): sources in sections 3. Gap Parakh fills: nobody does the lookup for a non-expert, in their language, inside WhatsApp.

### Inferences
- One-liner: "Forward it to Parakh: it checks SEBI, RBI, CBIC, Income Tax and the courts live, and replies in your language with the official source, not an opinion."
- Q1 "Why not just ChatGPT / WhatsApp Scam Alert?" Answer: they guess from text; Parakh retrieves live official records, returns a dated, linked verdict and abstains with "cannot verify" when a registry has no answer. WhatsApp bans general-purpose bots anyway; Parakh is a narrow verification service. Show a case where a generic LLM hallucinates a licence number and Parakh catches it.
- Q2 "What if the registry has no entry, is stale, or you mislabel a real firm? Defamation?" Answer: verdicts are statements of registry lookups with timestamp and link, not accusations; "not found" is shown as "not found, not proof of fraud"; the product never says "fraud/scam" about a named entity unless an official source (e.g. SEBI caution list, RBI alert) says so and is cited; registry data is not cached long-term. Cite RBI's own natural-justice standard as the bar for labelling.
- Q3 "How do you make money and handle privacy / scale?" Answer: users free; B2B white-label to banks, brokers, payment apps and state cyber cells priced per verification; chargeable in unit economics because WhatsApp user-initiated replies are near-free. Privacy: redact PII before LLM, store only hash + verdict, auto-delete, DPDP-ready before May 2027 obligations. Scale/risk: registry rate limits and scraping terms are the real constraint (state the plan: cache with TTL and source attribution, fall back to "cannot verify", and seek official API/data sharing from SEBI/RBI).
- Honest weak spots to pre-empt: no signed buyer, official APIs for eCourts/PIB not confirmed, Bhashini terms unknown, and the Supreme Court has not settled the fact-check-unit question.
