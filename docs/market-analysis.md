# Parakh targets a loss pool, not a market

**Parakh has a real, large problem to point at, an empty "authority-based" niche, and a thin set of registries it can legitimately call live in 48 hours.** Reported Indian cyber-fraud losses sit at about **Rs 22,500 crore a year** (2024 and 2025), with investment and trading fraud taking roughly three-quarters of the money, so "fake stock tip / fake adviser" is the highest-value target and "fake notice / digital arrest" is the most vivid one (about 9% of rupees) (secondary, MHA data via press). Government tools each check one thing after the user already knows which regulator is involved; private tools (Truecaller, Google, Airtel, small startups) judge by signals such as number reputation and link blacklists. In what the research found, none cites SEBI, RBI or Income Tax records, and none does so across several registries with a sourced verdict (survey finding, "none found", not "none exist"). The build constraint is sharper than the pitch constraint: only SEBI's intermediary list, AMFI, IRDAI lists and the eCourts form were confirmed reachable; almost everything else is captcha-, OTP- or bot-gated, so the honest design is a local cache of static lists plus deterministic format checks plus deep links. There is no documented paying customer for a consumer "forward-and-verify" tool, so revenue is a judgement call and the pitch should lead with the loss pool rather than a TAM. Legal exposure is manageable if Parakh reports "found / not found in a dated official record" and never calls a named firm a fraud; Meta's January 2026 ban on general-purpose AI chatbots makes a narrow-purpose framing mandatory. Every figure below carries a tier tag: **[P]** primary (none of the government figures were read in the primary document), **[S]** secondary (news quoting government), **[V]** vendor or survey, **[A]** analyst estimate, **[J]** judgement.

## Losses plateau near Rs 22,500 crore while complaints keep climbing

National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP) losses rose from about **Rs 2,290 crore in 2022** to **Rs 7,465 crore in 2023** and **Rs 22,845 crore in 2024** ([Morung Express](https://morungexpress.com/2268-lakh-cyber-crimes-registered-over-rs-22845-crore-lost-by-citizens-in-2024-centre); [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/07/22/del23-lsq-cyber-fraud.html), [S], Lok Sabha reply of 22 Jul 2025; the 2022 and 2023 rows are single-outlet). The 2025 total was **Rs 22,495 crore across 28.15 lakh cases**, up 24% in count but flat in rupees, meaning average loss per complaint fell from roughly Rs 1.0 lakh to Rs 0.8 lakh ([ThePrint, Feb 2026](https://theprint.in/india/cybercrime-saw-24-spike-in-2025-indians-lost-rs-22495-crore-mainly-in-investment-scams/2859930/), [S]; the per-complaint arithmetic is [J]). Only about 3% of cumulative financial-fraud complaints became FIRs, and 2025 FIRs actually fell (55,484 vs 66,370) (same source; the cumulative 65.89 lakh complaints / Rs 55,050 crore / 1.96 lakh FIRs figure comes from an uncaptured outlet, so cite ThePrint only). Surveys say reporting understates harm further: LocalCircles found about 6 in 10 Indians do not report financial fraud ([Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/technology/localcircles-survey-says-39-indian-families-claim-to-have-fallen-victim-to-online-financial-fraud-1155790), [V], undated and unclear whether 2023 or 2026).

Several of these numbers conflict, and the pitch should pick one source and name it. For 2025, other outlets give about 24 lakh financial-fraud complaints with the same Rs 22,495 crore ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/over-24-lakh-cyber-complaints-22495-crore-fraud-reported-in-2025), [S]); the 24 vs 28 lakh gap probably reflects financial-fraud-only vs all cases. A harder conflict is IANS citing I4C for **Rs 19,813 crore and 21.77 lakh complaints** ([IANS](https://ianslive.in/indians-lose-over-rs-52976-crore-to-cyber-frauds-over-six-years-report--20260103154943), [S]), which Patrika attributes to McAfee instead ([Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/patrikaplus/cyber-fraud-india-data-safety-tips-20868714)); it is unresolved and likely a part-year or narrower cut. Cumulative totals also disagree: Rs 36,448 crore for 2021 to Feb 2025 ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1080247/over-38-lakh-cyber-fraud-complaints-since-2021-most-from-up-maharashtra-centre), [S]) vs "over Rs 32,600 crore" reported from a December 2025 reply, which is lower despite a later date and so uses a different scope. The 2024 Hindi report of 19.18 lakh complaints and Rs 22,811.95 crore ([Aaj Tak](https://www.aajtak.in/technology/tech-news/story/cyber-fraud-cost-india-22812-crore-in-2024-tteca-dskc-2273960-2025-07-01), [S]) is another counting base. Do not mix them. Separately, the RBI Annual Report's FY26 bank-fraud total of **Rs 48,021 crore** ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/financial-institutions-report-over-10000-cases-of-fraud-involving-48000-cr-in-fy26-rbi-data), [S]) should not be used as a retail-scam number: Rs 30,199 crore of it is 314 old cases reclassified after a 2023 Supreme Court judgment. Its reported collapse in card and digital-payment frauds to 293 cases is implausible against the RBI Deputy Governor saying digital frauds have risen since July 2025 ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/digital-frauds-rising-since-july-2025-says-rbi-deputy-governor)), so treat it as unverified.

## Investment fraud is three-quarters of the money; digital arrest is the visible tip

By MHA's 2025 split, **investment fraud was about 76% of losses and 35% of cases**, digital arrest about 9% of money and 6% of cases, and sextortion 4% of money but 19% of cases ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/cybercrime-saw-24-spike-in-2025-indians-lost-rs-22495-crore-mainly-in-investment-scams/2859930/), [S]; rupee and case counts derived). That makes a stock-tip and adviser checker the best fit by value, while a notice checker fits the category the Supreme Court is watching: it took suo motu cognisance of digital arrest in October 2025, ordered a CBI probe in December 2025, and issued nationwide directions in August 2026 ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-orders-cbi-probe-into-digital-arrest-scams), [S]; the August order itself was not read). Digital-arrest figures conflict: 2024 is Rs 1,935 crore per MHA ([The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/03/12/del54-rsq-cybercrime.html)) but Rs 1,616 crore on 63,481 complaints in another report, and complaint counts differ by definition (1,23,672 in 2024 and 58,249 in 2025 from Supreme Court proceedings vs about 1.69 lakh implied for 2025 by ThePrint's 6% share) ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/whatsapp-banned-9400-accounts-used-for-digital-arrest-scams-in-2026-ag-tells-supreme-court/80326.html), [S]). Direction is clear even if levels are not: digital-arrest complaints appear to be falling (16,377 by June 2026), while the persistent category is investment fraud. SEBI itself warned in October 2026 about private WhatsApp tip groups with fabricated member endorsements ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/sebi-cautions-investors-against-fraudulent-stock-tip-whatsapp-groups), [S], press release not opened) and in February 2026 about forged STT notices ([IANS](https://ianslive.in/sebi-warns-investors-about-fake-stt-notices-account-handling-services--20260227153139)). Loan apps have no current national count; the oldest hard data is an RBI working group finding 600 of 1,100 apps illegal and Google removing 2,200+ in Sep 2022 to Aug 2023 ([GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/more-than-half-indian-loan-apps-illegal-rbi-panel-finds-a-17970); [CXO Digital Pulse](https://www.cxodigitalpulse.com/google-removes-2200-fraudulent-loan-apps-from-play-store-mos-finance/), [S], dated). There is no official split for fake income-tax, customs or court notices as a category, which is a gap Parakh cannot fill with official numbers.

Who is hit is only partly known. In 2025, seniors filed **1,03,488 complaints worth Rs 4,005 crore** (about 4% of complaints but about 18% of the Rs 22,495 crore, roughly Rs 3.9 lakh per complaint vs Rs 0.8 lakh overall) and women filed 4.63 lakh complaints worth Rs 3,765 crore ([Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/india/senior-citizens-women-lost-rs-7-769-crore-to-cyber-fraud-in-2025-articleshow-u9oad8w), [S]; ratios are [J] and the groups may overlap). That pattern supports an "adult child forwards on behalf of a parent" user story ([J]). The research found **no data on victims' language or city tier**, so any claim that vernacular users are hit harder is an assumption, not a finding. Survey data (all [V], small or vendor-run) paints the lures: GSMA's 2026 India report (n=500) has 12% scammed in the past year, with messaging apps the top channel at 42% ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/news/12-per-cent-of-indian-respondents-scammed-in-past-year-gsma)); LocalCircles found at least 42% of WhatsApp users received fake job offers ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/what-is-the-localcircles-survey-is-saying-about-whatsapp-scams-in-india)). McAfee's claim that **51% of Indians lost money** (average Rs 93,915, 13 scam messages a day) ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/general/indians-get-13-scam-messages-daily-ai-and-deepfake-boosting-scams.html/amp/), [V]) sits far above GSMA's 12% and above anything official; use neither as a headline. On how people verify today, the best data point is that only **27% of GSMA victims called back on an official number**; no survey was found on first-resort behaviour (family vs Google vs bank vs ignore).

## The gap is cross-registry, document-in, with a sourced verdict

Every official tool answers one narrow question. Sanchar Saathi's Chakshu reports suspicious calls and messages but returns no verdict, and its "Trusted Contact Details" feature is Beta and limited to banks ([Sanchar Saathi](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in), official). RBI Sachet is a manual name lookup across RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA registration ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/personal-finance/thinking-of-investing-rbi-s-sachet-lets-you-verify-scheme-before-you-trust-125111201164_1.html), [S]). RBI's public list of digital lending apps exists as policy but is "as is, without any further validation" by RBI ([RBI press release 60403](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60403), quoted via search). SEBI Check and "@valid" UPI handles, live from about 1 October 2025, verify only the payee ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/10/04/sebi-introduces-verified-upi-handles-and-sebi-check-to-safeguard-investors/), [S]); SEBI also runs a document-number authenticator. The Income Tax "Authenticate notice/order" page needs a DIN or notice metadata and an OTP ([ITD help page](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/authenticate-noticeorder-issued-itd), official, quoted), and PIB Fact Check covers claims about the Government of India, not personal financial notices ([PIB FAQ](https://pib.gov.in/FAQ_fact.aspx)). The I4C suspect registry is aimed at banks and police; a public NCRP suspect-search is described only in secondary sources. Roughly eight portals each hold one slice, and only the Income Tax and SEBI tools check an actual document, only by an identifier the user must find and type ([J]).

Private tools are crowded at the signal layer and empty at the authority layer. Truecaller launched an Android Scam Checker in India in late September 2026 for links and numbers, with screenshot upload "expected" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/27/truecaller-takes-its-scam-intelligence-to-the-open-web-as-it-looks-beyond-caller-id/), [S]); Google claims Messages scam detection on about 500M messages a month, 41M Google Pay warnings in 2024 and a public lending-app list built with RBI ([Google blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/company-news/googles-safety-charter-for-indias-ai-led-transformation/), vendor-reported); WhatsApp began a limited rollout of on-device scam-chat alerts from August 2026 ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/whatsapp-scam-alert-uses-ai-to-catch-chat-scams), [S]). Small Indian startups (Kavach, ScamMukt at about Rs 50 a month, ScamDekho, Bachao.AI, Seerie) are paste-a-link-or-number products whose registry use and language support are undocumented ([Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/business/startups/bengaluru-startup-launches-kavach-app-to-flag-suspicious-calls-messages-4093714); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/scammukt-launches-indias-dedicated-ai-scam-protection-app-because-spotting-a-scam-is-now-a-basic-survival-skill-for-every-indian/), listings). Abroad, Singapore's ScamShield is the best government analogue ([scamshield.gov.sg](https://scamshield.gov.sg/set-up-scamshield-app)) and Norton Genie the closest "paste it and ask an AI" product ([Norton Genie](https://genie.norton.com)); neither was found querying national regulator registries. The closest hackathon cousin is ScamGuard on Devpost, which integrates SEBI SCORES and the 1600 helpline ([Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/scamguard), listing). Smart India Hackathon and Devfolio archives were not searched, so "unoccupied" is unproven. Defensibility risks to say out loud: Truecaller, Google or WhatsApp can add paste-and-check quickly, and SEBI, which runs its own hackathon, could bless or build a competitor ([J]). The safe slide claim is "none found in our survey", not "no existing tool".

## Only a handful of registries can run live in 48 hours

The build sheet was tested on 31 Oct 2026 from a fetcher of unknown location, so failures may be geo- or bot-blocks rather than closed doors. **Confirmed reachable**: SEBI's registered investment-adviser list (1,071 records, "as on date Oct 30, 2026", HTML table, 25 per page, JavaScript paging, no export, no captcha on search; its robots.txt allows the page) ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/other/OtherAction.do?doRecognisedFpi=yes&intmId=13)); SEBI's orders listing (12,018 records, no last-updated stamp) ([SEBI orders](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=2&ssid=9&smid=6)); AMFI's distributor search by name or ARN ([AMFI](https://www.amfiindia.com/locate-distributor)); the IRDAI site with list pages (formats not opened) ([IRDAI](https://www.irdai.gov.in/)); and the eCourts form, which needs an image captcha and has no API ([eCourts](https://services.ecourts.gov.in/ecourtindia_v6/)). **Failed**: all three RBI pages (HTTP 418, likely bot filtering), BSE (403), MCA (403), NSE (timeout), PIB Fact Check (OTP login wall), cybercrime.gov.in (title only), UGC (maintenance text). **Documented but untested**: SEBI Check (the guessed URL did not resolve), the RBI Alert List of unauthorised forex platforms (95 names after the November 2025 update, with RBI stating absence does not mean authorised) ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/invest/check-rbis-updated-alert-list-for-unauthorised-forex-trading-platforms)), the RBI digital-lending-app list (live path and format unconfirmed), CBIC DIN verification (captcha, live URL unconfirmed because sources name three different hosts) ([GST Gyaan](https://gstgyaan.com/quoting-of-din-on-documents-issued-by-officers-of-cbic)), and the GST taxpayer search (captcha) ([GST tutorial](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/taxpayersdashboard/Search_Taxpayer_manual.htm)). One note conflict: the business note says no SEBI Check tool was confirmed, while the registry and competitor notes found it in several news reports; treat it as real but with an unverified URL. No official public API was found for any of these, and none appeared in API Setu or data.gov.in. The Income Tax check sends an OTP to the user's own mobile, so it cannot be automated by anyone else.

The design that follows is [J]: scrape politely and cache SEBI's roughly 1,100 adviser rows once, download the RBI, UGC (32 fake institutions in the Feb 2026 list, per [Kashmir Life](https://kashmirlife.net/ugc-identifies-32-fake-universities-across-india-states-asked-to-take-legal-action-446704/)) and IRDAI lists by hand into SQLite, add deterministic validators (GSTIN checksum, CIN pattern, eCourts CNR format, DIN format, the rule that an adviser or broker payment handle not ending in "@valid" is a red flag), and hand off captcha or OTP tools as deep links. Show a "fetched on" date for every source and word negatives as "not found in list fetched on <date>". Avoid third-party captcha-solving scrapers found in search; they circumvent the gate and would be an easy judge or legal objection. Site terms of use were not read for any registry, so a human should check them before scraping.

## Payers are unproven; telecoms and platforms are distributors, and the rules shape the wording

No source documents a bank, insurer, broker or payment app paying a third party for a consumer forward-and-verify bot. What does exist is large-scale distribution by others: Airtel claims 71 billion spam calls blocked and awareness reach above 600M (company-reported) ([Business World](https://businessworld.in/article/airtel-introduces-ai-powered-spam-detection-system-to-shield-customers-from-unwanted-calls-sms-535145), [V]); Google claims 17.7 crore reached through its Safety Charter and Digikavach ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/google-launches-online-safety-charter-to-protect-indians-from-digital-fraud-1.500166540), [V]); Chakshu reports led to about 39.43 lakh mobile disconnections ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/chakshu-under-sanchar-saathi-reports-fraud-39-43-lakh-mobiles-disconnected-over-1-000-crore-loss-prevented), [S], partial year). Pressure on banks is rising: 66% of surveyed Indian consumers think banks should always reimburse scam victims ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/two-thirds-of-bank-customers-in-india-expect-reimbursement-for-scam-losses-survey), [V], FICO 2024). The plausible payer order is bank, NBFC, broker or payment app as white-label "verify before you pay"; then state cyber cells or I4C as a free public-interest deployment; then CSR; telecoms, Google and Meta are integration partners ([J]). Market-size reports are enterprise fraud-detection, not this product: MarketsandMarkets has India at about $990M in 2026 growing to $2.7B by 2031 (the page also lists an inconsistent $35.7B base) ([MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/fraud-detection-prevention-market/India), [A]) and IMARC about $1.4B in 2024 ([IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/report/en/india-fraud-detection-prevention-market), [A]); these differ by a wide margin and a Businessworld "76 bn" headline has unclear currency, so quote none as Parakh's TAM. I4C's roughly Rs 1.2 lakh crore projection for 2025 ([Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/current-affairs-news-analysis-editorials/news-analysis/28-10-2024/print/manual), [A]) is a forecast contradicted by recorded losses of about Rs 22,500 crore, so do not use it.

Regulation cuts three ways. **Defamation and labelling**: the RBI's July 2024 fraud master directions require a show-cause notice with at least 21 days to respond before a bank classifies an account as fraud ([AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/master-directions-on-fraud-risk-management-in-banks-and-nbfcs), [S]), which is a useful benchmark for why Parakh should not brand named entities. No case law on chatbots labelling firms as scams was found, and the guidance here is [J], not legal advice: output classes such as REGISTERED, NOT FOUND, MISMATCH, PATTERN-MATCH and CANNOT VERIFY, each with a dated source link, and the word "fraud" only when an official source such as a SEBI caution or the RBI Alert List says so. Registry absence is weak evidence (stale lists, name variants, self-reported lending-app data). **DPDP**: the Rules were notified in mid-November 2025 and most duties apply after 18 months, around 12 to 13 May 2027 (sources differ by a day) ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/breaking-govt-notifies-final-dpdp-rules-sets-staggered-rollout-with-key-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries-84201.htm); [AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/?p=87799), [S]), so core duties are not yet binding at the demo but will be within about six months. Build for purpose limitation and erasure now: redact Aadhaar, PAN and account numbers before any LLM call, keep a hash plus verdict rather than the forwarded file, and show a consent line ([J]). **WhatsApp**: Meta's terms, enforced 15 January 2026, bar providers whose primary service is a general-purpose AI chatbot, while task-specific uses stay allowed ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/?rand=324); [Respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)). Parakh must describe itself as a narrow verification service. Per-message costs come from third-party guides (utility about Rs 0.11 to 0.16, free inside an open 24-hour service window, 18% GST, plus BSP markup) ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [V]); Meta's own rate card was not reached, and per-check cost is unverified until measured in the demo. India's WhatsApp base is about 535M by a third-party estimate (others range 487M to 853M) ([Hyperleap](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/whatsapp-statistics-india-2026), [A]), so say "hundreds of millions". Bhashini offers 22-language text translation and 12-language speech ([UNICEF](https://www.unicef.org/digitalimpact/bhashini-ai-making-languages-more-accessible-digital-technology)), but developer terms and limits were not found. One more caution: PIB Fact Check is a source, not an arbiter, because the IT Rules 2023 fact-check unit was struck down by the Bombay High Court in 2024 and the Supreme Court agreed to hear the appeal in March 2026 without a stay ([Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/court-updates/supreme-court/refused-stay-bombay-high-court-order-striking-down-it-rules-2023-fact-check-units-1609570)); the final outcome was not found.

## What judges will ask, and what Parakh can honestly say

The three questions below are predicted from the rubric (engineering 20, agentic 25, impact 25, demo 30), not from event evidence ([J]). First, "why not ChatGPT or WhatsApp's Scam Alert?": generic models guess from text, while Parakh retrieves dated official records, links them, and abstains with "cannot verify" when no registry answers; stage one demo where a generic LLM invents a licence number and Parakh does not. Second, "what if the registry is stale or you mislabel a real firm?": verdicts are lookup statements with a timestamp and link, "not found" is never "fraud", and the labelling bar follows the RBI's own natural-justice standard. Third, "how do you make money, handle privacy, scale?": free to users, per-verification pricing to banks, brokers, payment apps and cyber cells, PII redaction and delete-after-use, and a registry-access plan of caching with attribution plus a request to SEBI and RBI for data-sharing. Pre-empt the weak spots: no signed buyer, no confirmed official API for eCourts or PIB, Bhashini terms unknown. The evidence on past winners is thin (SIH 2025's software winner solved a phishing-detection statement, [KJSCE](https://kjsce.somaiya.edu/en/view-announcement/1043)); the inference that winners attach to government-owned problems with official numbers and a working end-to-end demo is [J].

## What this means for our build

| Decision | Recommendation | Why (tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Connect live | SEBI registered-intermediary list (fetch once with low rate, cache, show "as on" date); eCourts CNR and Income Tax DIN only as format validation | Only SEBI confirmed reachable, no captcha, robots.txt allows (tested) |
| Load as local lists | RBI forex Alert List and NBFC list, RBI digital-lending-app list, UGC fake list, IRDAI lists, AMFI ARN | RBI pages blocked the fetcher (418), so download by hand in a browser; stamp each with a fetched date (J) |
| Offline validators | GSTIN checksum, CIN pattern, CNR format, DIN format, "@valid" rule for adviser or broker payment handles | No network needed, cannot fail on stage (J) |
| Deep links only | eCourts, Income Tax authenticate, CBIC DIN, GST search, cybercrime.gov.in, PIB Fact Check, NSE and BSE pages, 1930 | Captcha, OTP or bot-gated; automating them is the ethics and legal trap |
| Demo delivery | Web or WhatsApp sandbox number showing a forward, with a web fallback | Meta approval risk (J); frame as narrow verification service |
| Languages | Hindi plus one more, via the LLM; Bhashini only if sign-up is quick | Bhashini terms not found |

Claims to avoid: no "no existing tool does this" (say "none found in our survey"); no "X is a scam or fraud" about a named entity unless an official source says so; no "checks eCourts, CBIC and PIB live" unless you actually query them, since none has a confirmed API (say "validates the identifier and sends you to the official check"); no TAM figure, no "51% of Indians lost money", no Rs 1.2 lakh crore, and no single loss number without naming its source; no signed-buyer language, and no claim about victims' language or city tier, which no source supports.

## Conclusion

The sharpest result is that the market case and the build case point at different products. The money is in investment and adviser fraud, which SEBI's one confirmed-reachable registry covers directly, while the emotionally powerful "fake court or tax notice" demo can only be live at the identifier-validation level, because every official check for it is gated on purpose. A credible Parakh is therefore a registry-backed router with a dated receipt, not a live-automation showpiece, and the honest framing is stronger than overclaiming because judges can probe a captcha-gated registry in seconds.

Two open items would move the pitch most. First, confirm with a human on an Indian connection whether the RBI, NSE and BSE pages work outside the test fetcher, and read site terms before scraping. Second, resolve which loss figure to cite (Rs 22,495 crore via ThePrint/MHA is the most widely reported, the Rs 19,813 crore figure unresolved) by opening the Lok Sabha replies named in the research notes, since no government figure here was read in a primary document.
